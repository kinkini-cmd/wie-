About Us

About SLTC IEEE WIE

The IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE) Student Branch Affinity Group of Sri Lanka Technology Campus (SLTC) is a student-led community committed to inspiring, supporting, and empowering individuals in Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM).

As part of the global IEEE Women in Engineering community, we strive to create opportunities for technical development, leadership, professional growth, and collaboration.

Through technical workshops, flagship events, competitions, mentoring initiatives, and community outreach programs, we encourage students to explore emerging technologies, develop their potential, and become future innovators and leaders.

Our Flagship Initiative

InspiHER{Tech} – Our Flagship Technical Initiative

InspiHER{Tech} is the flagship technical initiative of IEEE WIE SLTC, designed to inspire innovation, encourage technological exploration, and empower university students with industry-relevant knowledge and practical skills.

Through a series of technical workshops, interactive challenges, and hackathon experiences, InspiHER{Tech} provides a platform for students to explore emerging technologies, collaborate with fellow innovators, and transform ideas into meaningful solutions.

Covering areas such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Quantum Computing, and Programming, the initiative aims to bridge the gap between academic learning and practical technological experience.

Our Projects & Events

Creating Impact Through Our Initiatives

Explore the projects and events through which SLTC IEEE WIE promotes technical knowledge, professional development, innovation, and community engagement.

WiSTEM

A STEM outreach initiative that inspires school students through career guidance, interactive learning experiences, and educational activities, encouraging them to explore opportunities in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics.

BlenderOps

An international hands-on workshop introducing undergraduate students to 3D modelling, rendering, and animation using Blender, promoting creativity, technical knowledge, and practical learning.

IEEE WIE Day '26

A virtual celebration of IEEE Women in Engineering Day, featuring an inspiring keynote session, interactive activities, and opportunities for knowledge sharing and engagement among STEM undergraduates.

VioletGlow '26

A special celebration commemorating the 7th anniversary of SLTC IEEE WIE, bringing members together through engaging activities, entertainment, and networking experiences.
