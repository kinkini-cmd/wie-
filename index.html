About Us

About IEEE WIE SLTC

The IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE) Student Branch Affinity Group of Sri Lanka Technological Campus (SLTC) is a vibrant community dedicated to inspiring, supporting, and empowering women pursuing careers in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM).

As part of the world's largest international professional organization for advancing technology, we create opportunities for students to develop leadership skills, build technical expertise, engage in innovative projects, and connect with industry professionals.

Through workshops, hackathons, mentoring sessions, community outreach initiatives, and networking events, we strive to inspire every student to reach their fullest potential while promoting diversity, inclusion, and excellence in engineering.

Programs

Featured Projects & Events

WiSTEM

A humanitarian STEM outreach initiative that inspired students from underprivileged schools through career guidance, interactive learning activities, and educational resource donations, promoting STEM education and empowering future innovators.

BlenderOps

An international hands-on workshop introducing undergraduate students to 3D modelling, rendering, and animation using Blender, fostering creativity, technical skills, and innovation.

InspiHER{Tech} V4.0

The flagship technical initiative of IEEE WIE SLTC, featuring workshops, technical challenges, and a hackathon to equip university students with practical skills in Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Computing, and programming while promoting innovation and collaboration.

IEEE WIE Day '26

A virtual celebration of IEEE WIE Day 2026 featuring an inspiring keynote on STEM leadership, an interactive Kahoot challenge, and LinkedIn-shareable achievement badges, encouraging responsible innovation, leadership, and continuous learning among STEM undergraduates.

VioletGlow'26

A commemorative celebration marking the 7th Anniversary of the IEEE WIE Student Branch Affinity Group of SLTC. The event brought members together through interactive activities, entertainment, and networking, strengthening community spirit, collaboration, and member engagement.
